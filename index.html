About Kukatu

Kukatu is your comprehensive reading companion that brings together passionate readers in one beautiful, simple app. Whether you're looking to discover your next great read, connect with like-minded book lovers, or organize your reading life, Kukatu has you covered.

Features

📚 Book Discovery

Discover personalized book recommendations based on your reading preferences and community insights.

👥 Reading Communities

Join book clubs and discussion groups with readers who share your interests.

💬 Chat & Connect

Connect with fellow readers through our integrated messaging system.

📅 Reading Planner

Organize your reading goals and track your progress with our built-in planner tools.

📖 Reading Insights

Track your reading progress, set goals, and gain insights into your reading habits and preferences.

🛒 Book Shopping

Discover and purchase books through our affiliate partnerships and merchandise store.
