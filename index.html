About Kukatu

Kukatu gives your book club everything it needs to read together — from picking the next book to celebrating who read the most. Start a new club or join one with friends, set a yearly Club Challenge, and let a bit of friendly competition push everyone to read more.

Features

👥 Book Clubs, Reimagined

Create a club, invite friends, and set a Club Challenge — your group's yearly reading target to chase together.

📚 Track Your Reading

Log every book you finish. Browse millions of titles, scan barcodes, or import your Goodreads library in seconds.

🗳️ Vote on the Next Book

No more endless debates. Propose books to the club, let everyone vote, and the best suggestion wins.

🏆 Leaderboards

Every book counts toward your score. Set a bolder personal goal to earn more points, and see where you stand in real time.

📅 Events & Meetups

Plan discussion nights, set reminders, and manage RSVPs — all inside the app.

💬 Club Chat

Every club has its own group chat. Discuss plot twists, share quotes, and react in real time.

🔎 Friends & Discovery

Add friends, browse their shelves, and find your next favourite through the people whose taste you trust.
