10.1. Device Permissions & Data Usage

Kukatu requires certain device permissions to provide full functionality. You can manage these permissions in your device settings at any time.

Camera Permission:

  • Book Scanning: We use your camera to scan book barcodes (ISBN) for quick book lookup and addition to your library
  • Video Calls: Enable face-to-face communication with your book club members
  • Camera data is processed locally and not stored unless you save a book or join a video call

Microphone Permission:

  • Voice Messages: Record and send voice notes in book club chats
  • Audio/Video Calls: Participate in voice and video calls with club members
  • Audio is transmitted in real-time during calls and stored only for voice messages you send

Photo Library Permission:

  • Profile Pictures: Upload custom profile pictures
  • Club Pictures: Set custom images for your book clubs
  • Book Covers: Add custom book cover images
  • Photos are uploaded to our secure servers and associated with your account

Notification Permission:

  • Chat Messages: Receive alerts for new messages in your book clubs
  • Event Reminders: Get notified about upcoming book club meetings and reading deadlines
  • Reading Goals: Receive motivational updates on your reading progress
  • System Updates: Important app updates and announcements
  • You can customize notification preferences in app settings

Background Processing:

  • Message Sync: Keep your messages up-to-date
  • Notification Delivery: Ensure timely delivery of important alerts
  • Call Availability: Receive incoming calls when app is in background
  • Background processing is optimized to minimize battery usage

Network Access:

  • Real-time Messaging: Send and receive messages instantly
  • Content Sync: Keep your books, clubs, and planner synchronized
  • Media Upload/Download: Share images, voice messages, and book data

Important: All permissions are optional but some features may be limited without them. You maintain full control over these permissions through your device settings.
