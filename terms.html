Kukatu Ltd is the data controller for the personal data described here. We collect only what the app needs to work.

What we collect:
• Your email address and login details
• Your birth year, to confirm the 13+ minimum
• If you sign in with Google or Apple, the account identifier, email address, name and profile image that provider gives us
• Your profile settings and preferences
• What you do in the app, such as the books you record, the clubs you join, the messages you send and the ISBNs you scan
• What you choose to write, such as reviews, notes and highlights
• Crash and error reports

Why we collect it:
• To operate the app and keep your account working across devices
• To confirm the 13+ minimum and apply age-appropriate safeguards
• To find and fix faults
• To protect users and to comply with legal duties

How long we keep it:
• Your account data, and the messages you post in club chats, for as long as your account is open
• Direct messages and voice messages from before those features were withdrawn are kept with your account
• When you delete your account we remove your data straight away, and in any case within 30 days, apart from anything we are required to keep by law
• Messages you posted in a club chat may stay visible to that club after you leave or delete your account, so the conversation other members took part in remains readable

Who else receives it:
• Supabase, which provides the database, sign-in and file storage behind the app, and processes data on our instructions
• Google Firebase, which delivers push notifications and collects crash reports and app performance measurements on our instructions

• Wiktionary and dictionaryapi.dev, which receive a word you select while reading if you ask for its definition

The last three groups are independent services rather than providers acting on our instructions, and they receive only what is listed above. We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

How we protect it:
• Data is encrypted in transit and access is limited to the staff and providers who need it

Your rights:
Under UK GDPR you may access, correct or delete your data, ask for a copy of it, withdraw consent, object to processing, and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. You can export your data and delete your account from within the app at any time.

To exercise your rights, contact info@kukatu.co.uk.

If you hold rights in a work and believe Kukatu is making it available without permission, write to info@kukatu.co.uk with:
